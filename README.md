Coffee Shop Sales Analysis | SQL & Power BI
Project Overview
This project analyzes coffee shop sales data using SQL, Excel, and Power
BI to understand business performance, customer purchasing patterns,
product performance, and sales trends.

The project includes SQL-based analysis, an Excel dataset, and an
interactive Power BI dashboard. The goal is to convert transaction data
into actionable business insights that can support sales improvement,
inventory planning, and operational decision-making.
Business Objectives
- Track overall sales performance using key performance indicators
  (KPIs).
- Analyze sales contribution across store locations.
- Identify high-performing products and product categories.
- Understand sales patterns by hour, day, and month.
- Identify opportunities to improve off-peak sales and cross-selling.
- Support better inventory, staffing, and promotional decisions.
Tools & Technologies
- SQL: Querying and analyzing sales transaction data.
- Microsoft Excel: Dataset storage and review.
- Microsoft Power BI: Interactive dashboard development and
  visualization.

Dashboard Analysis & Key Insights
1. Sales by Store
Sales contribution is relatively evenly distributed across the three
stores:
- Lower Manhattan: 32.04%
- Astoria: 33.93%
- Hell's Kitchen: 34.02%
Business recommendation: Review store-level staffing, inventory, and
marketing strategies. Consider targeted promotions based on each
location's performance.
2. Sales by Product Size
- Large: 34.05%
- Regular: 28.59%
- Small: 6.64%
- Not Defined: 30.72%
Business recommendation: Improve product-size data completeness to
support more accurate reporting and inventory planning.
3. Hourly Sales
The report shows sales concentrated in the morning, followed by a
decline later in the day. It identifies the period after 6 PM as
off-peak.

Business recommendation: Test evening promotions, dessert offers, or
special events to encourage visits during slower hours.
4. Sales by Day
- Highest reported sales: Monday ($102K)
- Thursday: $101K
- Lowest reported sales: Saturday ($97K)
Business recommendation: Introduce weekend-specific offers, such as
Saturday specials or family deals, to improve weekend footfall.
5. Sales by Product Category
  Category        Sales    Share
  Coffee         $270K   38.63%
Tea          $196.4K   28.11%
Bakery        $82.3K   11.78%

Coffee is the largest revenue contributor, followed by tea and bakery.

Business recommendation: Introduce coffee-and-bakery combo offers
and promote complementary products to increase cross-selling.
6. Top-Selling Products
- Ethiopian Coffee: $42.3K
- Sustainably Grown Coffee: $39.1K
- Jamaican Coffee: $38.8K
Business recommendation: Promote specialty coffee products through
in-store displays, product storytelling, sampling, and loyalty offers.
7. Monthly Sales Trends
- Highest reported month: June ($166K)
- Lowest reported month: February ($76K)
Business recommendation: Plan seasonal promotions and limited-time
offers during lower-performing months.
Business Recommendations
1. Improve off-peak sales: Run evening and weekend promotions.
2. Increase cross-selling: Bundle coffee with bakery products.
3. Promote specialty products: Highlight popular coffee varieties
   through sampling and loyalty campaigns.
4. Plan seasonal campaigns: Target months with lower sales.
5. Optimize store operations: Use store-level performance to guide
   staffing, inventory, and marketing.
6. Improve data quality: Reduce missing or undefined product-size
   information.
